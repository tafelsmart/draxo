FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Draxo.

Is Draxo really undetected?

Draxo uses a multi-layered anti-cheat engine with packet scheduling, sine-wave jitter rotations, a randomization engine and position-based micro offsets. We regularly test against Watchdog (Hypixel), GrimAC, Vulcan and Spartan. No cheat is 100% undetectable — use Draxo responsibly and avoid obvious rage settings on servers with active staff.

How do I get a key?

Visit the keygen page, enter your HWID (copy it from the launcher: 📋 Copy), complete 3 short Linkvertise ads and receive your 24h key. The key is bound to your hardware (HWID-locked).

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

Draxo supports all versions from 1.17 to 26.x. The built-in mapping generator automatically creates the correct mappings for your version. That's 34 versions in total.

How does the installation work?

Download DraxoLauncher.exe and place it in any folder. The launcher automatically finds Python, installs dependencies and injects the DLL into Minecraft. No complicated configuration needed.

Can I play on Hypixel with my main account?

We strongly advise to never cheat on your main account. Use an alt account or test on servers like Minemen.club or Flamefrags.gg that are more tolerant towards clients. Hypixel bans permanently on detection.

What is the Simple/Advanced mode?

In Simple mode, every module has only 2 presets: Legit (safe, subtle) and Rage (maximum effect). Perfect for beginners. Advanced mode gives you full control over timing, range, jitter and all other parameters — for experienced users.

My key doesn't work — what should I do?

The most common cause is a wrong HWID: the key is hard-bound to the HWID of your machine, which you see in the Draxo launcher (📋 Copy). Always use the exact same HWID in the keygen — not one from an old draxo_config.ini or another PC. Other causes: the key is expired (24h), your system clock is wrong, or you generated a second key for the same HWID (rate limit). First check that the HWID matches exactly (32 characters, uppercase). If activation still fails, check the support box on the keygen page — it links to our Discord, where we fix HWID issues within minutes.
