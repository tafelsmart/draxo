How to find your HWID
Option 1 — Draxo Launcher
Start the DraxoLauncher.exe. In the license card between the control panel and console, you'll see your HWID. Click 📋 Copy and paste it here.

Option 2 — After first injection
Inject Draxo into Minecraft once. Your HWID is then saved in build/vanilla/Release/draxo_config.ini. Open that file and look for License.hwid=... — that's your 32-character HWID.

Important
The key is HWID-locked. Use the SAME HWID as in your Draxo Client — otherwise the key won't work.
